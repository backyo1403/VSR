✈️ How to Play — Sky Race

The race

12 legs from START to FINISH along an S-shaped route. Each leg, everyone answers one question within 20 seconds. The answer is revealed automatically the moment the countdown ends.

The race keeps going until at least 4 aircraft land on FINISH. Reaching FINISH shows you your own placing right away, even while the race continues for everyone else.

📍 Checkpoints

The five gold stops above are checkpoints. Whoever is first to reach HAN, SPC or SGN earns a milestone marker next to their name for the rest of the race.

🏆 Winning

First to FINISH wins. If several aircraft land on the same reveal, they're ranked by correct answers, then by total answer time — so answering fast matters.

🌈 Clear Skies

The default. Correct answer advances you 1 leg; a wrong answer holds your position — no penalty.

🌪 Turbulence

Correct answer holds your position. Anything else — wrong, or no answer at all — sends you back 1 leg. 50:50 and Turbo Boost are disabled; this is the only time Fasten Seatbelt works.

⛈ Storm

Only the 10 fastest correct answers get ATC clearance to advance 1 leg. Everyone else holds position, including correct answers outside the top 10 — but nobody goes backwards. All three helps and the Power Question are disabled.

☀️ Power Question

The host's one-time bonus round for the whole game, armed before the question opens. The fastest correct answer gets +2 legs, every other correct answer +1, wrong answers hold. Can't be armed on a Turbulence or Storm leg, and all three helps are disabled.

Your three helps

All three unlock the moment your own aircraft reaches MUC — not when the race reaches MUC. Each one can be used once per game.

50:50

Removes two wrong answers, leaving the correct one and a single decoy. Disabled during Turbulence, Storm and the Power Question.

Turbo Boost

Double or nothing. Correct → +2 legs. Wrong → −1 leg. Arming it costs nothing until you press Submit, so you can change your mind. Disabled during Turbulence, Storm and the Power Question.

Fasten Seatbelt

Your turbulence shield — and it only works during Turbulence. Ride out a wrong answer without being pushed back a leg. Useless otherwise, so hold on to it until turbulence hits.
